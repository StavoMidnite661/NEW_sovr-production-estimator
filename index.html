Calculation: material = quantity × material rate × (1 + waste) × material market index. Labor = quantity × labor hours/unit × hourly rate × (1 + labor burden). Equipment = quantity × equipment/unit × material market index. The market index is applied to material and equipment only — labor is set by the hourly rate and burden, since the two markets diverge. Tax uses the configured taxable material/equipment basis. Contingency and overhead apply to positive direct base cost before credits; profit applies to direct cost plus overhead; escalation applies to direct cost and configured adders. Verify contract/accounting policy. Credit rows reduce base cost; alternates and owner-supplied rows stay outside the base total.
